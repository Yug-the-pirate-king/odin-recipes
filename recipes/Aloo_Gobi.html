Aloo Gobi

Description

Aloo Gobi is a quintessential vegetarian dish that is a staple in Indian households and restaurants worldwide. The name literally translates to "Potatoes" (Aloo) and "Cauliflower" (Gobi). It is a dry curry, meaning the vegetables are sautéed in spices rather than swimming in a heavy gravy.

What makes a great Aloo Gobi is the texture; the cauliflower should be tender and infused with spices like turmeric and ginger, but still retain a slight crunch. It’s a healthy, comforting, and flavorful dish that pairs perfectly with hot rotis or as a side to dal and rice.
