lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a quintessential Italian comfort food consisting of wide, flat pasta sheets layered with a rich meat or vegetable ragù, creamy béchamel or ricotta cheese, and plenty of melted mozzarella and parmesan. It is baked in the oven until the edges are crispy and the cheese is bubbling.

While there are many variations, the most famous is Lasagne alla Bolognese, which hails from the Emilia-Romagna region of Italy. It’s a labor of love that is perfect for feeding a crowd or meal-prepping for the week.

Ingredients

  • 1 box of lasagna noodles (oven-ready or traditional)
  • 500g ground beef or Italian sausage
  • 1 jar (approx. 700ml) of marinara or bolognese sauce
  • 400g ricotta cheese
  • 1 large egg (to bind the cheese)
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Fresh basil and dried oregano

Steps

  1. Prep the Noodles: If using traditional noodles, boil them in salted water until al dente. If using oven-ready, set them aside.
  2. Make the Meat Sauce: Brown the ground meat in a skillet with garlic. Drain the excess fat and stir in your marinara sauce. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Prepare the Cheese Mixture: In a medium bowl, combine the ricotta cheese, egg, 1/2 cup of mozzarella, and herbs. Mix well.
  4. Assemble the Layers:
    • Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in the bottom of a baking dish.
    • Place a layer of noodles on top.
    • Spread a layer of the ricotta mixture over the noodles.
    • Top with more meat sauce and a handful of mozzarella.
  5. Repeat: Continue layering until all ingredients are used, ending with a thick layer of mozzarella and parmesan on top.
  6. Bake: Cover with foil and bake at 190°C (375°F) for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 15 minutes until the cheese is golden and bubbly.
