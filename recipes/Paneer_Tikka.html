Paneer Tikka

Description

Paneer Tikka is a popular Indian appetizer made from chunks of paneer (Indian cottage cheese) marinated in spiced yogurt and grilled in a tandoor or oven. It is a savory, smoky, and charred delicacy that is a favorite at parties and restaurants.

Traditionally served with mint chutney and onion rings, this dish is known for its bright orange hue and the perfect balance of soft paneer with crunchy onions and bell peppers.

Ingredients

  • 250g Paneer, cut into thick cubes
  • 1 large oioin and 1 bell pepper,cut into squares
  • 1 cup thick greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • Spices: Kashmiri red chili powder, turmeric, and chaat masala
  • 1 tsp Kasuri Methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
  • 1 tbsp Mustard oil (for that authentic smoky flavor)
  • Lemon juice and fresh coriander for garnish

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, whisk the yogurt with all the spices, ginger-garlic paste, and mustard oil to create the marinade.
  2. Gently add the paneer cubes, onions, and bell peppers to the bowl. Coat them thoroughly and let it rest for at least 30 minutes.
  3. Thread the marinated paneer and vegetables onto skewers, alternating between them.
  4. Grill in a preheated oven at 200°C for 15-20 minutes, or pan-sear on a tawa until the edges are charred and golden.
  5. Sprinkle with chaat masala and a squeeze of lime before serving hot.
