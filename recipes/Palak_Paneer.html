Palak Paneer

Description

Palak Paneer is one of the most beloved vegetarian dishes in North Indian cuisine. It consists of paneer (Indian cottage cheese) cubes served in a thick, smooth gravy made from pureed spinach (palak). The dish is not only delicious but also packed with nutrients from the fresh greens.

Unlike many other paneer dishes that are tomato-based, Palak Paneer focuses on the earthy flavors of spinach tempered with garlic, ginger, and green chilies. It is best enjoyed with garlic naan, laccha paratha, or simple zeera rice.

Ingredients

  • 500g fresh spinach (palak), washed and blanched
  • 200g paneer, cubed (fresh or lightly pan-fried)
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chilies, slit
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
  • Spices: Turmeric, garam masala, and a pinch of asafoetida (hing)
  • 2 tbsp heavy cream or malai (optional, for richness)
  • 1 tbsp butter or ghee

Steps

  1. Blanch the Spinach: Boil water, add spinach for 2 minutes, then immediately plunge it into ice-cold water to retain its bright green color.
  2. Puree: Blend the cooled spinach with green chilies into a smooth paste.
  3. Sauté: Heat ghee in a pan, add cumin seeds, and sauté onions until translucent. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook until fragrant.
  4. Simmer: Pour in the spinach puree and add the spices. Let it simmer on low heat for 5-7 minutes.
  5. Finish: Gently fold in the paneer cubes and stir in the heavy cream. Simmer for another 2 minutes and serve hot with a dollop of butter.
